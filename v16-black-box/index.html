Playbill · The companySeason 8 — 2026–27

Saanjh Repertory is a resident company of fourteen actors, two writers and one very tired stage manager, working out of a converted printing press in Sector 10, Chandigarh. We opened in 2019 with borrowed lights and forty folding chairs.

Saanjh means dusk, and it also means something shared. We make new plays for a room where nobody can hide, the actors least of all. Every production is new writing or newly devised, and every Thursday is pay what you can.
