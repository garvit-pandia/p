Playbill · BiographyVol. 16 — 2026

Garvit Pandia is an engineer & builder based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India. He holds a B.Des Creative Technology from Northern Arc Institute of Design & Technology (2021–2025, GPA 3.6/4.0), where the type-design coursework taught him that measurement is a creative act.

His company credits span games, editorial systems, and developer tools — 46 projects shipped, 21 clients, 31 mentees, 6 hackathon wins, 9 talks, and 3 papers. He believes tools should be quiet: they should work, then get out of the way.
