      05 Behind the counter

      Where your sample goes all day.

      A report is only as good as the twelve hours before it. This is what happens between the needle and the message on your phone.

      1. 6:30 AM

        Collected

        One needle, one try. Your name and a barcode go on the tube while you are still in the chair.

      2. 9:00 AM

        At the bench

        Tubes are checked against the slip, then spun so the serum separates from the cells.

      3. 11:30 AM

        On the analyser

        Control samples run first. If the controls are off, nothing of yours is run until they are right.

      4. 4:00 PM

        Read and signed

        The pathologist reads every report. Anything far out of range is repeated before it is signed.

      5. 7:00 PM

        With you

        A PDF on your phone and a printed copy at the counter. Urgent values get a phone call first.

      09 Before you come

      Asked at the counter daily.

      Do I need to fast before my test?

      Only for tests marked as fasting on the list, such as fasting sugar and the lipid profile. Water is fine. Tea, coffee and breakfast are not. Ask your doctor whether to take your morning medicines before or after the sample.

      How do I get my report?

      As a PDF on your phone and as a printed copy at the counter, whichever you prefer. You can follow the sample with the number on your slip in the meantime.

      What does home collection cost?

      ₹60 within 4 km. It is free when your tests come to ₹800 or more, and it is included in the Sixty Plus package.

      Do I need a prescription to buy medicine?

      For prescription medicines, yes. A clear photo is enough to place an order, and we check the original when we deliver.

      How can I pay?

      Cash, UPI or card, at the counter or to the person who delivers. Nothing is charged before the sample is taken or the medicine is handed over.

      Will someone explain my report to me?

      Yes. The general physician goes through any report made here at no charge on your next visit. For anything urgent we call you before the report is sent.
