## 5. V15 — The Impossible Transit Map

Folder: `v15-transit-map`
Display name: `THE IMPOSSIBLE TRANSIT MAP`.

Concept:
A transit network for ideas, systems, skills, and experience rather than a literal city map.
The visitor follows routes through Garvit's work.

Visual direction:

- warm map paper
- cobalt and ultramarine route ink
- vermilion transfer marks
- graphite station labels
- pale blue water/empty map fields
- subtle registration marks and folded-map edges

Hero:
Multiple route lines enter from different edges, make impossible but legible intersections, and
resolve into the title. Include a clear station legend and a primary route.

Interaction:

- scroll traces the route through the page
- the active section becomes a station with a transfer pulse
- station clicks smooth-scroll to sections
- project cards behave like line stops and show their connected route/skill labels
- route hover highlights only the related path, not the entire page

Sections:

- Origin Station — About
- Interchange — Projects
- Signal Yard — Skills
- Transfer Records — Achievements
- Long Route — Experience
- Terminus — Contact

Do not make this a flat infographic. Add depth through paper, route layering, station plates,
subtle map folds, and scroll choreography while keeping all content readable.
