01

Foundry

Who draws these — 2026

Typevolt is three type designers and one engineer in a room above a print shop. We have released eight families in seven years, which is slow, and on purpose. Every typeface is named after an electrical unit, because a font is the same thing: a small standard that everything else gets measured in.

Focus

TEXT
FIRST

We draw the paragraph before the poster. If a face cannot set six hundred words comfortably, it does not get a bold.

Method

SPACE,
THEN KERN

Most of a typeface is the white between letters. We spend more hours on spacing than on drawing, and it shows at small sizes.

Belief

FEWER,
BETTER

One release a year, finished. No font we sell has a version number that starts with zero.

Proof

ON PAPER

Every style is proofed on a laser printer, a risograph and the worst phone screen we can find before it ships.
