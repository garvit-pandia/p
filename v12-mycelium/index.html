One organism, many fruiting bodies

I’m Garvit Pandia — a systems & data engineer who builds tools that keep growing in the wild: storage that self-heals, forecasts farmers trust, messages that arrive on bad networks. I studied B.Sc Computer Science at Uttaranga Institute of Applied Sciences, where my independent research on agri-sensor data became the seed of everything since — 8.8/10, and that sensor work still feeds my field projects today. My work spreads along three hyphae: resilient distributed systems (Rhizome survives 30% node churn with zero data loss), environmental data (Kelp warns of acidification six days early), and open source — 176 PRs merged, 31 first-time contributors mentored to their first merge. Wherever the network spreads, I mentor: office hours by terminal, and judging rubrics that teach as they score.
