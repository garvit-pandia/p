Portfolio Concepts v4

Fourteen faces of Garvit

One resume, fourteen completely different worlds — 01–06 are the originals, 07–10 the new generation, 11–14 the genesis wave (abstract: black hole, mycelium, origami, radio signal). Every one is a fully working, animated portfolio. Open them, scroll, play — then tell me which one feels like you.

01

Sunlit Archive

Warm editorial light theme. Parchment, terracotta, drifting 3D paper petals that part around your cursor, preloader, side dot-nav, duotone project photography. Pin-able marginalia + hidden-thought notes. Light · Editorial 02

Midnight Index

Deep-space dark. 3D starfield + orbiting rings, a constellation that connects to your cursor, self-typing terminal, scanline deep-field panel. Star-drift parallax + zoom-drift starfield. Dark · Cosmic 03

Dream Loom

Living aurora shader in raw WebGL2, kinetic typography, floating blob photography, a sky that shifts as you scroll — and a button to shuffle the sky. Living-palette sections + thread tension. Wild · Fluid 04

Mono Factory

Neo-brutalist. Hard shadows, 2px borders, zero radius, giant Anton type, a terminal that types itself, marquee tickers and hover inversions. Interactive factory console + command flicker. Brutalist · Mono 05

Glass Horizon

Premium glassmorphism. Luminous gradient sky, drifting orbs, floating 3D glass prisms (Three.js), frosted panels with earned blur. 3D tilt glass cards, tilt on proof/form panels. Glass · Luminous 06

The Field Journal

Scroll-driven storytelling. Pinned parallax scenes, a horizontal snap-through project gallery, a timeline that draws itself as you scroll. Press-able specimens + collection tally. Scrollytelling · Editorial 07

Voxel World

A tiny isometric voxel diorama in Three.js — every section is an island (monument, lighthouse, city, skill tree, trophy mesa, railway, beacon). The camera flies island to island as you scroll; day turns to night. Signature: clickable voxel progress rail. 3D · Voxel 08

The Abyss

A bioluminescent dive through the work — canvas plankton, jellyfish, light rays and sonar pings; a depth rail that descends to 11,204 m (Challenger Deep). Projects are glowing specimen jars. Signature: hover a specimen and it pings. Dark · Bioluminescent 09

Ghostware

A holographic memory archive — rotating particle hologram, scanlines, live fake telemetry, ARCH-serial memory slots that glitch-load on click. Signature: the ACCESS:// command bar — type "help", "recall arch-0001", "jump work". Cyber · Hologram 10

Typevolt

The kinetic editorial machine — pure type, zero canvas. Giant Anton lettering that choreographs itself with scroll, case-file rows, spec-sheet skills, magnetic buttons, a cursor that speaks. Signature: heading letters shy away from your pointer. Editorial · Kinetic 11

Event Horizon

The portfolio as a black hole — you are falling in. Scroll = descent through rings of the accretion disk; content spirals inward, warps and accelerates as you descend. Canvas-2D disk with dust spiraling to the singularity. Signature: gravity. Dark · Cosmic 12

Mycelium

The portfolio as a living fungal network. Projects are fruiting bodies, skills are enzymes, achievements are spores — hyphae grow between sections as you scroll. Nothing appears; everything grows. Signature: growth. Dark · Organic 13

Origami Aurum

A warm paper origami gallery — parchment, terracotta, gold foil. Every section unfolds like a paper flap; folded-paper letters, dog-eared project cards, stamp-grid achievements. Zero canvas: the light perf showcase. Signature: the fold. Light · Paper 14

SIGNAL//DECODE

An intercepted cosmic transmission. The name decodes from static letter by letter; a spectrogram gains signal as you scroll; every section locks in with a [ DECODED ] stamp. Transmit back through the contact form. Signature: decode. Dark · Radio
Built with HTML / CSS / JS · Three.js (r160, InstancedMesh) · Canvas 2D · zero frameworks · vision-model audited & scored (round 6: heroes up to 9.5/10; genesis wave v11–v14)
