Log 002 — The school

About the school

Depth 5 m — the lagoon

Paatal is the old word for the world underneath. We opened in 2016 with one boat and two instructors, on a beach where the reef starts thirty metres from the door. We are still small, and we mean to stay that way.

The rule the school is built on: never more than four divers to one instructor, and two for a first dive. It means we turn people away in January. It also means nobody here has ever been hurried through a skill they were not ready for.

Most of our students arrive nervous and a third arrive unable to swim well. You do not need to be brave. You need to be able to breathe slowly, and we can teach you that on the sand.

    Log 007 — Your first four days

    Open Water, day by day

    Depth 36 m — nearly there

    Day 1 · the classroom and the lagoon

    Breathe, clear, float

    A morning on how pressure works and how the gear fits. After lunch you kneel on sand in chest-deep water and breathe underwater for the first time. Nobody goes deeper until that feels ordinary.

    Days 2 and 3 · Turtle Garden, 12 m

    The first real dives

    Two boat dives a day. You practise each skill once at the surface and once on the reef: clearing a mask, sharing air, hovering without your hands. The rest of the time you look at fish.

    Day 4 · The Staircase, 18 m

    You plan it, we follow

    Your buddy and you plan the last dive yourselves: depth, time, air, the way back. Your instructor stays behind you and says nothing. You surface a certified diver.
