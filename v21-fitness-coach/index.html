The coach

I count your reps, not your excuses.

I was a state-level sprinter until my back gave out at twenty-four. Learning to lift properly fixed it, and I have been teaching that ever since.

Most of the people I coach sit at a desk for ten hours. They do not need a transformation. They need to pick up their kids, climb stairs and sleep well, for the next forty years.

Four programs.

Each one runs in twelve-week blocks with a test day in week six. Not sure which is yours? The finder below takes thirty seconds.

01

Base

Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. The five movements, done properly, with weight that goes up.

Intensity 3/560 min
02

Burn

Sleds, ropes, bikes and short rests. For when the goal is a lighter body and a bigger engine.

Intensity 5/545 min
03

Flow

Hips, shoulders, spine. Slow strength at the ends of your range, so the rest stops hurting.

Intensity 2/550 min
04

Hybrid

Heavy lifts twice a week, hard conditioning twice a week. For people who want both and can recover.

Intensity 4/575 min
